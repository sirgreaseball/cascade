<h3 align="center">The first hours, on one screen</h3>

<p align="center">
  Pick any of 144 Indian dams, choose how it fails, and watch the flood run down the real valley, minute by minute.<br>
  Arrival times, depths and the people in the water update as it moves.
</p>

<img src=".github/media/views.webp" width="100%" alt="Depth while the flood computes, the hazard to people, and the peak depth along the valley">

<br>
<br>

<h3 align="center">Two solvers. One honest answer.</h3>

<p align="center">
  Cascade works out the same flood with two independent methods.<br>
  Where they agree, the answer holds. Where they disagree, the gap is drawn on the map,<br>
  so uncertainty is never hidden behind one confident colour.
</p>

<br>

<h3 align="center">Not only dams</h3>

<p align="center">
  Glacial-lake outbursts, controlled releases and cloudbursts run on the same engine,<br>
  over real terrain, real roads and real settlements, from Tehri to the 1979 Machchhu II failure at Morbi.
</p>

<br>

<h3 align="center">Built to be trusted, not to impress</h3>

<p align="center">
  Every number on screen carries its caveat. Cascade is a planning and exercise tool for the people<br>
  who write evacuation plans. It is not a forecast and not an official warning.
</p>
